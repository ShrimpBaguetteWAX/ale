Become a trainer

You're a trainer: you recruit alien fighters, send them into fights, and turn wins into TLM and Shards. Getting started takes four steps.

1Connect your walletLog in with your WAX wallet.
2Pick a trainer nameRegister once and choose the name other players see.

4Follow the BriefingIt shows your next step, starting with recruiting five fighters.

Understanding fighters

Every fighter has a codex page: open one from My Fighters, the Market or a fight. Here's one, tap anything on it to see what it means.

Building your team

Every fight takes exactly five fighters plus a sixth: the NFT fighter, built from one Alien Worlds crew card and one weapon card. Tap fighters to put them in your team; rest the pointer on one to read it first.

Your team0/5
Your team · 0/5
  • The crew card sets the NFT fighter's class, race and targeting; the weapon sets its element. Their stats and abilities add up.
  • Order only matters on a tie: among equals, the fighter placed earlier acts first.
  • A fighter can't join while it's busy (on the Market, defending an arena, in a tournament) or while its payday is overdue.

How a fight plays

Fights are automatic and settled on the chain; the battle screen is a replay. Whoever has the lowest wind-up acts next; after striking, its wind-up grows by its cooldown. Step through it:

Every blow comes in the attacker's element, and the target's resistance to that element cuts it. There's no element chart: resistances decide.
  • Each fighter picks targets by its own targeting rule (for example Highest Taunt or Lowest Health). Taunt draws enemy blows and drops each time the fighter is hit.
  • On a tie in dungeons and arenas, your side acts first. The last team standing wins; a fight still going after 1,000 blows is a draw.
  • Health and damage grow with level and fade with age. The land's weather can change stats for both teams.

Quests

Three daily, three weekly and three monthly quests. Finish one, claim it, and its TLM or Shards go straight to your wallet.

  • A claimed quest is replaced by a new one straight away.
  • Don't like a quest? Re-roll it for credits.
  • Each quest has its own deadline: it stays open until the end of the next day, week or month. If it runs out unfinished, it's gone.
  • Without a Legend Pass, quest rewards are reduced.

Hall of Fame

The champion, the runner-up and the two beaten semi-finalists, who share third, are written into the Hall of Fame on the chain, for good.

  • Prizes arrive in Rewards: claim them there.
  • Without a Legend Pass, both prizes are a tenth.
  • With fewer than two trainers signed up, the tournament is called off.

Leaderboards

Dungeons

A rating built by winning dungeons that fades a little each day. The top places can claim a reward once a day (UTC), paid in TLM and Shards; higher places get more.

Arena

Runs in seasons. You earn points while your fighters hold arenas, more for higher-rated arenas and for holding several. When a season ends the top places share its pot, credited to Rewards, and the board starts over.

Your profile also ranks every lifetime stat against other trainers, just for bragging rights.

Farming

Put the Alien Worlds cards you're not using to work. Stake tools, crew and weapons in the farm, and claim credits from its three pools.

Farming pools right now ● liveCredits

15% of every credit anyone spends in the game flows into these pools, on top of a daily top-up.

  1. Stake cardsSend tools, crew or weapons to the farm: 1 gem per card.
  2. Let it growEvery day, your cards' weight adds to your share of each pool.
  3. ClaimOne claim pays credits from all three pools at once.
  4. Unstake any timeYour cards come back to your wallet, no fee.

Ascension

Level 10 is the top, but your best fighters can still grow. Give up three fighters of the same class and your fighter gains an upgrade, for good. There's no limit: ascend it again and again.

Your Arcanists · tap three to sacrifice try it

✦5 opens a hidden ability

Every fighter carries a third ability it can't use yet. It stays locked until its fifth ascension, then fights alongside the other two in every battle, for good. This is the big reason to keep ascending.

  1. Reach level 10Only a fighter at the top level can ascend.
  2. Pick three sacrificesSame class: one same race, one same element, one with the Sacrifice ability.
  3. Ascend999 credits. The three are gone for good.
  4. Choose an upgradeOne of three random offers, or re-roll them for 499 credits.
  • Upgrades are small but permanent, and they stack: usually a main stat (damage, health, cooldown, wind-up or taunt), sometimes a resistance.
  • Each ascension adds a ✦ to the card and 10 points to your tournament team score.

Market

Buy and sell fighters with other trainers, for gems. Every sale is a 48-hour auction: the highest bid when the clock runs out wins.

The 1-gem listing fee is paid when you list, sold or not. The 15% sale fee only comes off when it sells.

  • Your bid's gems are held while you're on top, and come straight back the moment someone outbids you. My listings shows the auctions you've been outbid on.
